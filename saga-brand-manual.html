SAGA already has the hard parts: an all-electric fleet, company-owned cars, salaried pilots and a flat ₹18 per km fare. What it lacked was a brand that tells anyone this. This system fixes that.

SAGA · Positioning04
02 · Positioning

Not a cab app. The coast's first predictive, electric mobility company.

Aggregator apps react: you ask, they search, you hope. SAGA plans ahead. Your ticket number, your weekly routine or your company's roster books the ride before you think about it. Owning the cars and employing the pilots is what makes that promise possible, and it is what no aggregator can copy.

Positioning statement

For travellers, families and organisations across Visakhapatnam and its nearby towns, SAGA is the electric mobility company that plans your ride before you need it. Unlike aggregator apps, every SAGA car is company-owned and every pilot is a salaried employee, so pickups are locked, prices are flat and nobody gets stranded.

Four brand pillars

A

Anticipation · We move first

Ticket-based airport booking, daily rides and shift dispatch. We earn trust by acting early and showing how we worked it out.

B

Pure energy · Every km is electric

Riders see the CO₂ they avoided after every ride, and it adds up to free rides.

C

Quiet precision · Calm and exact

No hype, no exclamation marks. Times to the minute, fares to the rupee.

D

Coastal pride · Built for Vizag first

Eastern Ghats, the Bay of Bengal, Telugu first. Future-facing, and from here.

SAGA · How we stand out06
03 · Six proof points

Six features that make the difference. Each one is hard to copy.

01

SAGA Fly · Book from your ticket

Type the PNR once. SAGA reads the flight, times the pickup and moves it if the flight is late.

02

Assigned rides · No cancellations

Dispatch assigns every ride to a salaried pilot. Riders never watch "searching for drivers" fail.

03

Flat fare · ₹18 per km, always

The fare on screen before you book is the fare you pay. Rain, festivals and midnight included.

04

Secure 360 · Safety you can see

The pilot starts the ride only after your trip code. Alerts are reviewed by a person, never by AI alone.

05

SAGA Saathi · Book by voice

Speak in Telugu, Hindi or English. Built for people who find apps hard, not only people in a hurry.

06

Green points · See the good you do

Every ride shows the CO₂ you avoided. Points add up to free rides.

Why no one else can do this

Aggregators don't own cars or employ drivers, so they can't guarantee a pickup, hold a price or plan charging. SAGA's model is the moat; the brand's job is to make it obvious.

09 · Voice & tone

One voice. The tone changes with the moment.

Our voice never changes: calm, exact, warm. Our tone adjusts to what the person is going through. A booking confirmation can smile; a safety message must not.

1

Fact first

"Pickup moved to 04:42. Your flight is 25 min late."

2

One person

"Ravi is at Gate 2." Not "your driver partner has arrived".

3

Short

Headlines under 12 words, messages under 25.

4

Same words

"Trip code", "pilot", "green points", everywhere.

09 · Words and languages

Three languages, one voice.

Telugu, Hindi and English are written by people, not machine-translated. Telugu copy uses everyday spoken Telugu. Respectful forms are the default (మీరు, आप). Numbers, times and prices stay in Western digits in every language so they scan at a glance.

Words we use

Pilot · trip code · pickup · arrive · planned · electric · green points · on time · we're sorry · thank you

Words we avoid

Driver partner · surge · oops · hurry · limited time · revolutionary · AI-powered in headlines · emoji in the app · ALL CAPS

SAGA · Notifications17
11 · Notifications

Fewer messages. Each one useful.

Every notification answers one question the rider would otherwise have to ask. If it doesn't, we don't send it.

Rules
  • Title: the fact, under 40 characters. Body: what it means, under 90.
  • Times, plates and prices as numbers.
  • Ride messages are never mixed with offers.
  • No offers between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m.; at most 2 a week.
  • Every message opens the screen it's about, in the rider's language.
Priority and channel
  • Critical · SOS and safety: push, SMS and a call, sound on.
  • Ride · pickup and arrival: push and lock-screen live activity.
  • Planned · reminders and flight changes: push and WhatsApp.
  • Account · receipts and points: in-app and email.
  • Offers · opt-in only, in-app and WhatsApp.

SAGA · Rewards & advertising29
21 · Green points and advertising

Rewards that mean something. Ads that never feel like noise.

How points work

1 point = 100 g CO₂ avoided compared with a petrol cab. Points expire after 12 months.

What they buy

2,000 points: a free airport ride and priority daily-ride slots. 6,000: upgrades and partner experiences.

Honest claims

Always "estimated CO₂ avoided", never "zero emissions". The method is published in the app.
