What changed in v4

Black and blue heroes, live 3D, a real driver app.

Type

Inter Tight + JetBrains Mono

Inter Tight for every word people read; JetBrains Mono for live data such as ETA, battery, km, plates, codes and CO₂, so numbers read like instrument readouts.

Colour

Black #07080C with SAGA Blue #092EC9

Black and blue carry the heroes, the tab bar, the telemetry panels and the HUD. White stays the reading surface, so older riders can still read easily in daylight. Cyan #33C6F0 is reserved for live/tech moments.

Icons

Lucide, one stroke

All interface icons come from Lucide 0.344 (2 px stroke) for one consistent, legible set.

Motion

GSAP, purposeful and tech

Odometer-style digit rolls, route dash-flow, pin drops, radar lock-on, a receipt that prints, leaf particles and spring presses. Every animation is forced to its end state if frames stall, so screens are never left half-drawn.

Live 3D

Interactive service objects

The service cards now hold live Blender objects (blender/saga_modules_glb.py). You can drag them, and they react to the controls: day discs light up, the outstation route extends, the sun moves for each trip, and towers light up per car.

Operations

The driver starts the ride

The rider tells the pilot a 4-digit code and the pilot types it in the driver app. The rider's screen moves from On the way to At pickup to Ride started by itself. There is no "start ride" button for customers.

Driver app simulation: SAGA Pilot. Shift check, assigned job, 3D night navigation, trip-code keypad, live trip with a Secure 360 alert, charging and shift summary.

All three apps on one screen: SAGA Suite. Rider, driver and ops console side by side and linked live. A booking on the rider phone reaches Ravi's phone, and his acknowledge, arrival, code entry and trip end move the rider's screen and appear in ops.

SAGA Saathi, the voice assistant (rider app): a mic button opens a sheet in English, हिंदी or తెలుగు. Riders can book, confirm, track the car, book an airport ride from a PNR, set up daily rides, outstation, day trips and business rides, check green points, call the driver, cancel or send SOS, all by voice. The demo simulates the listening and understanding. In production, speech goes to Gemini (speech-to-text plus function calling) with the same intent list as tools, and it can only call the app's own actions. The driver app has no voice assistant by design.

What changed in v3

Light, bold, ride-hailing first, built around the real car.

Interface

Light theme, one blue

The rider app moves to a light, high-contrast interface for older riders. The brand blue is #092EC9, used as bold full-colour heroes (Pilo-style) and for every primary action. Type is Plus Jakarta Sans: 17 px body, 34 px headings, 800 weight for headlines.

Home

A ride first

Home opens as a normal ride-hailing screen: a live map with SAGA cars moving nearby, "Where are you going?", and saved places. The other services sit below as swipeable cards with working controls inside: PNR, weekdays, distance slider, trip picker, people stepper.

Vehicle

The real SAGA ONE

Remodelled in Blender from the sagamobility.com side profile: a compact 5-door EV hatchback, 4.27 m long, gloss black, with roof sensor pods, a cyan LED window outline and sill line, LED wheel rings, a checkered taxi mark and red vertical tail lights.

Ride

Live 3D route

During the ride the app shows a light 3D city. The actual car model (exported as GLB) drives the route ribbon with turning wheels and a sensor pulse, while the HUD shows the next turn, arrival time, speed, battery and CO₂ saved.

Trust

Know your car

Before pickup: number plate, colour and lights to look for, battery and range, the lifetime CO₂ this car has saved, features, the pilot, the trip code, and a rotatable 3D model of the car.

Care

SAGA Assist

A ride type for older riders and families: the pilot helps with bags and boarding and waits longer. Sample price: +₹30.

02 · Positioning

Not a cab app. The region's first predictive, electric mobility company.

先回り (sakimawari): to get there first, so everything is ready when the other person arrives.

This idea from Japanese hospitality sits at the centre of SAGA. Aggregator apps react: you ask, they search, you hope. SAGA anticipates. Your PNR, your weekly routine or your company's shift roster books the ride before you think about it. Owning the fleet and employing the pilots is what makes that promise possible, and no aggregator can copy it.

Positioning statement

For travellers, families and organisations across Visakhapatnam and its tier 2 and 3 towns, SAGA is the electric mobility company that plans your ride before you need it. Unlike aggregator apps, every SAGA car is company-owned and every pilot is a salaried employee, so pickups are locked, prices are flat and nobody gets stranded.

2.3 · Where we're headed

From a cab service to the way the coast moves.

The long-term direction is a predictive mobility network for Andhra Pradesh's coastal cities and the towns around them. Every step builds on the same three assets: owned electric cars, employed pilots, and data that lets us plan rides before people ask.

Now · 2026–27

The most trusted ride in Vizag

Airport, daily rides and business accounts. 200 cars. Voice booking in three languages. On-time above 98%.

Next · 2027–28

Across the coast

Kakinada, Rajahmundry, Vizianagaram and Srikakulam. Intercity Voyages with planned charging. Hospital and campus partnerships.

Later · 2029+

A mobility network

Shared shuttles on predicted routes, connections to rail and buses, and fleet charging that also supports the grid. Ready for driver-assist technology, with pilots kept in the car.

4.1 · Voice & tone

One voice. The tone changes with the moment.

Our voice never changes: calm, exact, warm. Our tone adjusts to what the person is going through. A booking confirmation can smile; a safety message must not.

Four voice rules

1 · Say the fact first

"Pickup moved to 04:42. Your flight is 25 min late."

Not: "Good news! We've got an update about your ride…"

2 · Speak to one person

"Ravi is at Gate 2."

Not: "Your driver partner has arrived at the pickup location."

3 · Short, then shorter

Under 12 words for a headline, under 25 for a message. One idea per sentence.

4 · Same words everywhere

"Trip code", "Pilot", "Green points". The same term in the app, on WhatsApp, on the phone and on the car.

Three languages, one voice

Telugu, Hindi and English are written, not machine-translated. Telugu copy uses everyday spoken Telugu, not formal textbook Telugu. Respectful forms are the default (మీరు, आप). Numbers, times and prices stay in Western digits in every language so they are easy to scan.

4.2 · Tech with a conscience

Loaded with technology. Led by people.

SAGA uses AI, live 3D, voice and cameras. That only builds trust if people can see that the technology works for them and not on them. Three commitments keep the brand ethical, emotional and relatable.

Ethical

Fair by design

  • Pilots are salaried employees with weekends off, not gig workers chasing incentives.
  • No surge pricing. The fare does not rise when people need us most.
  • Safety alerts are always reviewed by a person. Pilots see their own alerts and can explain.
  • Data is used to plan rides, not sold. Camera footage is deleted after 7 days unless an incident is open.
  • The voice assistant can only do things inside the app, and always confirms before booking or paying.
Emotional

Moments, not transactions

  • The calm of knowing the 4 a.m. airport ride is handled.
  • A parent watching the school run arrive, live.
  • A grandparent booking alone, in Telugu, by voice.
  • A pilot proud of a safety bonus and a 4.96 rating.
  • Seeing the CO₂ you saved, ride after ride.
Relatable

Of this place, for these people

  • Landmarks, not coordinates: "Gate 2, near the temple".
  • Real pilots and real riders in campaigns, never stock models.
  • Prices compared to everyday life: "less than an auto at night".
  • Festivals, monsoon, exam season and the early-flight life are in our stories.
  • Tech explained the way you'd explain it to your parents.

4.3 · Notifications

Fewer messages. Each one useful.

Every notification must answer one question the rider would otherwise have to ask. If it doesn't, we don't send it.

Rules
  • Title: the fact, under 40 characters. Body: what it means and what to do, under 90.
  • Times, plates and prices in numbers.
  • Ride messages are never mixed with offers.
  • No marketing between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m., and at most 2 offers a week.
  • Every message opens the exact screen it is about.
  • Sent in the rider's chosen language.
Priority
  • Critical, safety and SOS: push, SMS and a phone call, sound on.
  • Ride, pickup and arrival: push and live activity on the lock screen.
  • Planned, reminders and flight changes: push and WhatsApp.
  • Account, receipts and points: in-app and email.
  • Offers: in-app and WhatsApp only, opt-in.

09 · Glass system

Three thicknesses of glass, each with a job.

v2 uses glass sparingly: only for layers that float over content, such as the tab bar, the map sheet and toasts. Everything else is a flat graphite card with a hairline edge, which is calmer and easier to read.

VEIL · blur 12 · fill 4%Overlays & nav
PANE · blur 22 · fill 8→2%Default cards
SLAB · blur 26 · blue 40%Commitments
  • Veil is for things that float: tab bar, map chips, toasts.
  • Pane is for information: trip cards, KPIs, settings. It catches a moving highlight under the pointer or finger.
  • Slab is for money and commitments: fares, ride pass, monthly estimates. If a screen has a Slab, it has only one.
  • Never stack more than two layers of glass. Text on glass must reach a 4.5:1 contrast ratio. Test it on the brightest frame of the render behind.
  • Edges: 1 px Titanium at 11%, an inner top highlight at 16%, radius 22 px for cards and 18 px for buttons.
10 · 3D language

One studio-lit object per product, made in Blender.

v2 replaces the neon grid with a product-photography studio: a satin graphite floor falling off into black, one soft overhead light, frosted glass, satin titanium and a single blue element per object. Light-lines appear only as small LED signatures on the car. Each product keeps its own object, so a glance at the hero tells you where you are.

Render recipe

Sourceblender/saga_car_v3.py builds the real SAGA ONE, renders transparent front, side, rear and top views, and exports saga_one.glb for the app. saga_modules_light.py renders the service objects as transparent cut-outs for the light UI.
EngineCycles, GPU (OptiX), 112–192 samples, denoised. AgX view transform, Medium High Contrast look.
MaterialsFrosted glass (roughness 0.2–0.3) · Midnight clear-coat paint · satin Titanium · one satin SAGA Blue part. Emission scaled to 20%, used only for car LEDs and small cores.
GroundSeamless studio floor: satin graphite with a radial falloff into the black background.
Camera35–58 mm, slightly above the eye line. One hero object, centred or on the right third.
PostLight bloom on highlights only (threshold 0.7, strength 0.3), exported as JPEG on black. In the UI, fade with a vertical mask.

13 · Pilot app

A work tool for salaried pilots, not a gig marketplace.

Start of shift

Car, battery, checklist

Assigned car and depot, battery and range, and a four-point pre-trip checklist: tyres and lights, clean cabin, Secure 360 online, uniform. The shift can't start until every check is done.

Today's plan

The whole day, in advance

Dispatch plans every job: Fly pickups with live flight status, Loop and Business pools, and the charging slot. No bidding, no surge chasing.

Trip

Navigate and verify

Turn-by-turn on a dark map. The trip only starts when the rider's 4-digit trip code matches, so the right rider is in the right car.

End of shift

Rewarded for safe and on time

Trips, distance, on-time record, rating, safety-bonus progress and short training modules. The bonus rewards zero attention alerts, not trip volume.
